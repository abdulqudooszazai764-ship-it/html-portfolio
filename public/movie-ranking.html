The best movies according to Abdul Qudoos Zazai

My top 3 movies all time

Commando

Commando is an action movie about a brave soldier who uses his fighting skills to rescue a woman. I like it because of Vidyut Jammwal's martial arts, bravery, and impressive stunts.

War

War is an action thriller about two skilled soldiers who become enemies. I enjoy it because of its exciting fights, suspense, and amazing action scenes.

KGF

KGF is an exciting action movie about a man named Rocky who fights against powerful enemies to become a leader. I like it because of its story, courage, and powerful message about ambition.
